## Statement
Support configurable `Orchestrator` context delivery mode (`inline` vs `paths`) across `Policy` defaults (`default_context_mode`), `Task` frontmatter (`context_mode`), `mt prompt`, and `mt run` so agents can operate either with full self-contained instruction and file insertions or with concise path references.

## Impact Analysis
- Extends `policy/permissions.yaml` (`default_context_mode`) and `policy/schemas.yaml` (`context_mode` on `Task`).
- Updates `src/checkers/permissions_checker.py` to validate `default_context_mode` in `execution_environments`.
- Updates `src/orchestrator/prompt_builder.py`, `src/orchestrator/task_runner.py`, and `src/cli/main.py` to resolve `context_mode` (`inline` or `paths`), synthesize corresponding role prompts, and pass `MT_CONTEXT_MODE` during `Worktree` execution.
