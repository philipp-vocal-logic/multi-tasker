## Statement
Expand `Task` file budgets in `policy/schemas.yaml` (`max_write_files: 8`, `max_required_files: 10`), update `planner-agent` decomposition guidance in `src/multi_tasker/orchestrator/prompt_context.py`, and refactor `tests/` into single-concern modules below 400 lines without `_and_` filenames.

## Impact Analysis
- Supersedes `REQ-019` and deprecates `SPEC-019`, updating `TASK.max_write_files: 8` and `TASK.max_required_files: 10` in `policy/schemas.yaml`.
- Updates `render_decomposition_contract` in `src/multi_tasker/orchestrator/prompt_context.py` to guide single-concern `Task` decomposition without `_and_` test filenames.
- Splits mixed test modules into single-concern `tests/test_*.py` files below `refactor_target_file_lines: 400` and removes `test_traceability_and_cli.py` and `test_transitions_and_execution.py`.
