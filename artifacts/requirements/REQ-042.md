## Statement
When a stage agent emits a valid `Escalation` inside its `Worktree`, `run_agent_stage` shall merge the `Escalation` onto `BaseBranch`, route its status via `route_escalation`, transition blockable target artifacts (`REQ`, `SPEC`, `TASK`) to `blocked`, commit a traceable change, and return a `GATE_TASK_ESCALATED` `Finding`.

## Impact Analysis
Updates `src/multi_tasker/orchestrator/router.py`, `src/multi_tasker/orchestrator/stage_runner.py`, and `tests/test_stage_escalation.py`.
