## Statement
The `Orchestrator` and `mt` CLI must log all CLI invocations, `WorkerAgent` subprocess executions, and `MergeGate` evaluations into gitignored, mergeable JSONL files under `.report/logs/` without coupling logging to `Specification` documentation generation.

## Impact Analysis
- Adds `src/orchestrator/interaction_logger.py` to record structured JSONL events in `.report/logs/events.jsonl` and `.report/logs/tasks/<TASK-ID>.jsonl` while keeping `.report/` gitignored.
- Updates `main` in `src/cli/main.py` to log every `mt` CLI invocation with command arguments, resolved actor/task context, exit code, and elapsed duration.
- Updates `src/orchestrator/task_runner.py` and `src/orchestrator/worktree.py` to log `WorkerAgent` and `MergeGate` events and merge `Worktree` logs onto `BaseBranch` before `Worktree` removal.
