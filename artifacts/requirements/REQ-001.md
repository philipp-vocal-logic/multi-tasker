## Statement
Provide a deterministic `mt check` CLI suite that enforces `policy/*.yaml` rules across `permissions`, `schemas`, `glossary`, `limits`, `clean_code`, `architecture`, and `traceability` with zero output on success and single-line `Finding` output on failure.

## Impact Analysis
- Establishes the deterministic verification engine required by every `MergeGate`.
- Enforces vocabulary, conciseness, clean code, layer boundaries, and bidirectional `Traceability` on the repository itself.
