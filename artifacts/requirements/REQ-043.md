## Statement
`mt run --pipeline` shall forward `--max-workers` through `run_declarative_pipeline` and `_step_declarative_stage` to `run_scheduled_tasks` (defaulting to `1`) so `worker-agent` pipeline stages execute conflict-free `Task` batches with the configured concurrency.

## Impact Analysis
Updates `src/multi_tasker/cli/main.py`, `src/multi_tasker/orchestrator/stage_runner.py`, and `tests/test_stage_escalation.py`.
