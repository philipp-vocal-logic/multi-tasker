## Statement
Extend `mt approve` (`approve_artifact`) to support `Specification` (`SPEC-NNN`) artifacts alongside `Requirement` and `Decision` artifacts, and verify every `(agent_role, existing_state, target_state)` transition defined in `policy/permissions.yaml` and `policy/schemas.yaml` via data-driven tests.

## Impact Analysis
- Updates `approve_artifact` in `src/orchestrator/lifecycle.py` to accept `SPEC` artifacts and propagate dirty status to downstream `Task` artifacts.
- Adds data-driven `TestContract` functions in `tests/test_orchestrator.py` covering `SPEC` approval and all agent state transitions.
