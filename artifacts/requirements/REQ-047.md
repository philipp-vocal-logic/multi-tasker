## Statement
The `Orchestrator` and `MergeGate` must prepend `<worktree_path>/src` ahead of `<worktree_path>` in `PYTHONPATH` across all `Worktree` agent and verification subprocess environments, and must detect byte-identical overwrites of existing locked stage artifacts (such as Cycle-2 `REV-001.md`) so `normalize_new_stage_artifacts` restores the original file and allocates the next sequential artifact ID instead of failing with `GATE_EMPTY_DIFF`.

## Impact Analysis
Updates `src/multi_tasker/orchestrator/merge_gate.py`, `src/multi_tasker/orchestrator/prompt_builder.py`, `src/multi_tasker/orchestrator/stage_runner.py`, and `tests/test_pipeline_combinations.py`.
