## Statement
Provide CLI lifecycle subcommands (`mt initialize`, `mt intent`, `mt approve`, `mt resolve`, `mt run`, `mt trace`, `mt documentation`) backed by `src/orchestrator/` for end-to-end artifact state transitions, `Worktree` execution, `Traceability` inspection, and live specification generation.

## Impact Analysis
- Adds `src/orchestrator/lifecycle.py`, `src/orchestrator/lineage.py`, `src/orchestrator/documentation_generator.py`, and `tests/test_lifecycle.py`.
- Extends `src/cli/main.py` and `src/cli/output.py` with `initialize`, `intent`, `approve`, `resolve`, `run`, `trace`, and `documentation` subcommands.
