## Statement
The system must enforce `max_file_lines` (`500`) from `policy/limits.yaml` as the sole hard file-size limit in `TestContracts` and `MergeGate`, while introducing `refactor_target_file_lines` (`400`) as a non-blocking warning that surfaces refactoring targets in `specification-agent` and `planner-agent` prompts during the next planning cycle.

## Impact Analysis
- Adds `refactor_target_file_lines: 400` and `LIMIT_REFACTOR_HINT` remediation in `policy/limits.yaml`.
- Adds `collect_refactoring_targets(repository_root: Path) -> list[Finding]` in `src/checkers/limits_checker.py` and surfaces active refactoring warnings in `src/orchestrator/prompt_builder.py` for `specification-agent` and `planner-agent` prompts without failing `mt check limits` or `MergeGate`.
- Updates `tests/test_transitions_and_execution.py` to replace the hardcoded `360`-line assertion with `max_file_lines` (`500`) from `policy/limits.yaml` and verify `400`-line refactoring warning injection.
