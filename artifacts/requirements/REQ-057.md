## Statement
Enforce bidirectional parity between `pyproject.toml` dependencies, `allowed_third_party_packages` in `policy/code_architecture.yaml` and `DEFAULT_PYTHON_ARCHITECTURE`, and actual third-party imports and linters across `src/**` and `tests/**`.

## Impact Analysis
- Removes unused `"jsonschema"` from `allowed_third_party_packages` in `policy/code_architecture.yaml` and `DEFAULT_PYTHON_ARCHITECTURE` in `src/multi_tasker/orchestrator/lifecycle.py`.
- Adds a `TestContract` in `tests/test_architecture_checker.py` verifying that `pyproject.toml`, `policy/code_architecture.yaml`, `DEFAULT_PYTHON_ARCHITECTURE`, and AST imports in `src/**` and `tests/**` have zero unused or undeclared dependencies.
