## Statement
`run_declarative_pipeline` shall automatically retry review-rejected artifacts up to `max_review_cycles` with failed `ReviewRecord` context inlined and dispatch in-run `routed_to_agent` `Escalations` with `affected_artifacts` inlined, verified by a data-driven E2E test suite in `tests/test_pipeline_combinations.py`.

## Impact Analysis
Updates `src/multi_tasker/domain/parser.py`, `src/multi_tasker/orchestrator/prompt_context.py`, `src/multi_tasker/orchestrator/router.py`, and `src/multi_tasker/orchestrator/stage_runner.py`, and adds `tests/test_pipeline_combinations.py`.
