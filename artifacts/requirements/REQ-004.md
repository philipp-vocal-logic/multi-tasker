## Statement
Synthesize role prompts deterministically from live `Policy` and artifact files (`mt prompt`), enforce subprocess timeouts in `Worktree` and `MergeGate` runs, transition upstream `Intent` artifacts to `processed` upon `Requirement` approval, and validate `Task` commit `WriteScope` and `Intent` coverage in `mt check traceability`.

## Impact Analysis
- Adds `src/orchestrator/prompt_builder.py` and `mt prompt` CLI subcommand in `src/cli/main.py`.
- Extends `src/orchestrator/merge_gate.py`, `src/orchestrator/lifecycle.py`, `src/checkers/common.py`, and `src/checkers/traceability_checker.py`.
