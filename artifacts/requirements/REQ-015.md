## Statement
The `Orchestrator` shall enforce `ReviewAgent` (`review-agent`) verdicts (`pass` or `fail`) and `max_review_cycles` escalation at `review-agent` approval gates in `policy/pipeline.yaml`, validate `ReviewRecord` `target_artifacts` in `mt check traceability`, render `ReviewRecord` links in `.report/specification.md`, and log `merge_gate` events in `run_agent_stage`.

## Impact Analysis
- Updates `policy/pipeline.yaml` to add `max_review_cycles: 2` and configure `approval_gate: "review-agent"` on `requirement_to_specification` and `specification_to_tasks`.
- Updates `src/orchestrator/stage_runner.py` to evaluate `REV-*.md` verdicts, approve or ready passing artifacts, escalate exhausted reviews (`review_exhausted`), and record `merge_gate` events.
- Updates `src/checkers/traceability_checker.py` and `src/orchestrator/documentation_generator.py` to include `target_artifacts` from `REV-*.md`.
