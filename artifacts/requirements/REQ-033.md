## Statement
The `Orchestrator` role prompt synthesizer (`build_agent_prompt`) must replace verbose YAML `json_schema` dumps with compact Markdown frontmatter-and-section templates and a 3-line `Escalation` contract, include `allowed_internal_imports` in `Architecture Layers`, inline `Specification` `target_files` in `Scoped Target & Upstream Context`, and tailor `Execution Steps` Step 1 and engineering constraints to the active agent role.

## Impact Analysis
- Replaces raw `json_schema` YAML blocks and 55-line `ESC` schemas in `src/multi_tasker/orchestrator/prompt_builder.py` with compact frontmatter-and-heading templates while preserving `Artifact Limit` and section budget lines.
- Tailors `Execution Steps` Step 1 and `@traces` / source limit / review guidance per agent role, renders `allowed_internal_imports` per layer, and inlines `SPEC` `target_files` for downstream review and planning.
