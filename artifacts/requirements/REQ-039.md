## Statement
`Requirement` artifacts shall support an optional `supersedes` list of `REQ-NNN` IDs that `approve_artifact` automatically transitions (along with exclusively linked `Specification` artifacts) to `deprecated`, while `check_traceability`, `prompt_context`, and `lineage` exclude `deprecated` `Requirement` artifacts from active coverage and catalog checks.

## Impact Analysis
Updates `policy/schemas.yaml`, `src/multi_tasker/orchestrator/lifecycle.py`, `src/multi_tasker/checkers/traceability_checker.py`, `src/multi_tasker/orchestrator/prompt_context.py`, `src/multi_tasker/orchestrator/lineage.py`, and `tests/test_lifecycle.py`.
