## Statement
Enforce fail-fast and fail-clear execution for enabled `ruff` and `mypy` linters in `mt check clean_code` so missing linter executables or non-zero `mypy` exits never pass silently, and ensure all `TestContracts` pass `mypy --strict`.

## Impact Analysis
- Updates `_run_ruff` and `_run_mypy` in `src/multi_tasker/checkers/clean_code_checker.py` to emit `CODE_LINTER_MISSING` when an enabled linter binary is absent from `PATH` and `CODE_MYPY` when `mypy` exits non-zero without parsed file-line diagnostics.
- Updates `tests/test_stage_escalation.py` and `tests/test_clean_code_checker.py` so `mt check all` passes with `mypy --strict`.
