## Statement
Separate `policy/*.yaml` by single concern into 10 clearly named files (`agent_execution.yaml`, `agent_permissions.yaml`, `artifact_lifecycle.yaml`, `artifact_limits.yaml`, `artifact_schemas.yaml`, `code_architecture.yaml`, `code_limits.yaml`, `code_quality.yaml`, `glossary.yaml`, `traceability.yaml`) and update all checkers, `Orchestrator` modules, CLI, and `TestContracts` in lockstep.

## Impact Analysis
- Splits `policy/permissions.yaml` into `policy/agent_permissions.yaml` (access control) and `policy/agent_execution.yaml` (models, `execution_environments`, `worktree_execution`, and `clean_repo_invariants`).
- Splits `policy/schemas.yaml` and `policy/pipeline.yaml` into `policy/artifact_schemas.yaml` (`schemas`), `policy/artifact_limits.yaml` (`artifacts` budgets), and `policy/artifact_lifecycle.yaml` (`state_transitions`, `execution_bounds`, `stages`, `escalation_routes`).
- Renames `architecture.yaml`, `limits.yaml`, and `clean_code.yaml` to `code_architecture.yaml`, `code_limits.yaml`, and `code_quality.yaml`, updating all `src/multi_tasker/**` and `tests/**` consumers.
