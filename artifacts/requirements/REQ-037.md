## Statement
`approve_artifact` shall transition an upstream `Intent` to `processed` only when every `Requirement` tracing to that `Intent` is `approved` (keeping the `Intent` in `processing` while sibling `Requirements` remain unapproved), and `_step_declarative_stage` shall execute `ReviewAgent` verification across all produced stage artifacts before approving them.

## Impact Analysis
Updates `src/multi_tasker/orchestrator/lifecycle.py`, `src/multi_tasker/orchestrator/stage_runner.py`, `tests/test_lifecycle.py`, and `tests/test_stage_runner.py`.
