## Statement
`build_agent_prompt`, `mt prompt`, and `mt run` shall use a single self-contained prompt format that inlines schemas, section budgets, `Architecture Layers`, `Glossary` constraints, upstream context, and a prescriptive 3-step `Execution Steps` sequence (`mt check all --agent <role> ...`), removing `context_mode` (`inline` vs `paths`) branching across `policy/*.yaml`, `cli`, `checkers`, and `orchestrator`.

## Impact Analysis
Updates `policy/permissions.yaml`, `policy/schemas.yaml`, `src/multi_tasker/checkers/permissions_checker.py`, `src/multi_tasker/orchestrator/prompt_builder.py`, `src/multi_tasker/orchestrator/stage_runner.py`, `src/multi_tasker/cli/main.py`, and `tests/test_interaction_logging.py`.
