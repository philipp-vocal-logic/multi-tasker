## Statement
The system must align source code and artifact sizing limits (`max_function_lines: 50`, `max_function_args: 6`, `max_criterion_words: 45`, `max_write_files: 4`, `max_required_files: 6`) across `policy/limits.yaml`, `policy/schemas.yaml`, and `src/checkers/limits_checker.py`, and render top-level `artifacts.<TYPE>` limits and `single_paragraph_only` rules in `src/orchestrator/prompt_builder.py`.

## Impact Analysis
- Updates `max_function_lines: 50`, `max_function_args: 6`, and `LIMIT_FUNC_ARGS` remediation in `policy/limits.yaml` and fallback defaults in `src/checkers/limits_checker.py`.
- Updates `REQ.max_criterion_words: 45`, `TASK.max_write_files: 4`, and `TASK.max_required_files: 6` (plus matching `json_schema` `maxItems`) in `policy/schemas.yaml`.
- Updates `_render_artifact_schema_block` in `src/orchestrator/prompt_builder.py` to surface top-level `artifacts.<TYPE>` limits and `single_paragraph_only` section rules in synthesized role prompts.
