## Statement
`src/multi_tasker/orchestrator/lifecycle.py` shall be decomposed below `refactor_target_file_lines` (`400` lines) by moving `evaluate_review_verdict`, `_create_review_escalation`, and `resolve_escalation` into `src/multi_tasker/orchestrator/router.py` and updating callers in lockstep without re-exports.

## Impact Analysis
Updates `src/multi_tasker/orchestrator/lifecycle.py`, `src/multi_tasker/orchestrator/router.py`, `src/multi_tasker/orchestrator/stage_runner.py`, `src/multi_tasker/cli/main.py`, and `tests/test_stage_escalation.py`.
