## Statement
Provide a deterministic `Orchestrator` that manages clean `Worktree` isolation, enforces the `MergeGate`, schedules conflict-free parallel `Task` batches, computes incremental dirty subgraphs, and routes `Escalation` items.

## Impact Analysis
- Adds `src/orchestrator/` modules (`worktree.py`, `merge_gate.py`, `scheduler.py`, `router.py`).
- Extends `src/cli/main.py` with `mt schedule` and `mt impact` subcommands.
