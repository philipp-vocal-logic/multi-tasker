## Statement
Eliminate silent fallbacks and legacy policy shims across `checkers`, `domain`, and `orchestrator` so `ruff` formatting/checking and unknown execution environments fail fast and clear, and `state_transitions` and `escalation_routes` are read exclusively from `policy/artifact_lifecycle.yaml`.

## Impact Analysis
- Updates `format_python_files` (`src/multi_tasker/checkers/common.py`) and `_run_ruff` (`src/multi_tasker/checkers/clean_code_checker.py`) to emit `CODE_LINTER_MISSING` or `CODE_RUFF` when formatting or checking fails.
- Updates `run_agent_stage` (`src/multi_tasker/orchestrator/stage_runner.py`) and `_execute_single_task` (`src/multi_tasker/orchestrator/task_runner.py`) to emit `CLI_UNKNOWN_EXEC_ENV` when a requested execution environment cannot be resolved.
- Removes `fallback_policy` from `load_state_transitions` (`src/multi_tasker/domain/parser.py`) and removes `CATEGORY_ROUTES` from `src/multi_tasker/orchestrator/router.py` and `src/multi_tasker/orchestrator/prompt_builder.py`.
