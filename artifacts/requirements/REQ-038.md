## Statement
When `target_paths` are provided to `check_traceability`, `_check_requirement_to_specification_coverage` and `check_specification_target_files` shall scope their completeness checks to the `Requirement` and `Specification` artifacts referenced by `target_paths`, while checking all repository artifacts when `target_paths` is `None`.

## Impact Analysis
Updates `src/multi_tasker/checkers/traceability_checker.py`, `src/multi_tasker/checkers/code_and_git_traceability.py`, and `tests/test_traceability_and_cli.py`.
