## Statement
Enforce validate-before-mutate semantics in `approve_artifact` so failed approvals never write `status: approved` to disk, and eliminate redundant `BaseBranch` `Task` re-serialization in `_finalize_task_outcome` when `MergeGate` verification fails.

## Impact Analysis
- Updates `approve_artifact` and `_finalize_task_outcome` in `src/orchestrator/lifecycle.py`.
- Adds `test_atomic_approve_and_failed_run_preserve_clean_base` in `tests/test_orchestrator.py`.
