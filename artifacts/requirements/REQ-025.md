## Statement
The `Checker` runner and `Permission` validator shall fail fast with `PERM_TASK_ID_MISSING` whenever `worker-agent` omits `--task` (even when `changed_files` is empty), forward `target_paths` to `check_permissions` in `run_single_check`, exclude the `in_progress` `Task` file when auto-detecting `Worktree` diffs for `worker-agent`, remove dummy `task_id` fallbacks in `task_runner.py`, and render explicit `--task` flags in `worker-agent` prompts.

## Impact Analysis
Affects `src/checkers/runner.py`, `src/checkers/permissions_checker.py`, `src/orchestrator/prompt_builder.py`, `src/orchestrator/task_runner.py`, and `tests/test_traceability_and_cli.py`.
