## Statement
Provide a declarative logic pipeline in `policy/pipeline.yaml` defining bounded `execution_bounds`, stage triggers (`stages`) with `approval_gate` (`human`, `review-agent`, `auto`), and `escalation_routes`, enforced by `check_schemas` and executed via `mt run --pipeline`.

## Impact Analysis
- Adds `policy/pipeline.yaml` as the single source of truth for stage transitions, approval gates, and `Escalation` routing.
- Updates `src/domain/parser.py`, `src/checkers/schemas_checker.py`, and `src/orchestrator/router.py` to load, validate, and enforce `policy/pipeline.yaml`.
- Updates `src/orchestrator/stage_runner.py` and `src/cli/main.py` (`mt run --pipeline`) to step through ready stages and `routed_to_agent` `Escalation` records in isolated `Worktrees`.
