## Statement
The `Orchestrator` shall programmatically calculate next sequential `Artifact` identifiers, inject assigned identifiers and scoped verification/decomposition guidance into role prompts, normalize newly created stage `Artifact` identifiers and intra-batch references prior to `MergeGate` evaluation, defer `TRACE_MISSING_TEST_CONTRACT` while `TestContract` `Tasks` are still `planned` or `ready`, and handle stage timeouts and multi-artifact review gates cleanly.

## Impact Analysis
Affects `src/domain/parser.py`, `src/checkers/code_and_git_traceability.py`, `src/orchestrator/prompt_builder.py`, `src/orchestrator/stage_runner.py`, `src/orchestrator/lifecycle.py`, and `tests/test_checkers.py`.
