## Statement
The `Orchestrator` and `Checker` infrastructure shall scaffold `__pycache__/`, `*.py[cod]`, and `.pytest_cache/` into `.gitignore` during repository initialization and filter those ephemeral paths in `get_git_changed_files` so bytecode and test runner caches never trigger `PERM_OUTSIDE_WRITE_SCOPE` in a `Worktree`.

## Impact Analysis
Affects `src/orchestrator/lifecycle.py`, `src/checkers/common.py`, and `tests/test_checkers.py`.
