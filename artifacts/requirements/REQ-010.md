## Statement
The pipeline must automatically execute successive dependency waves in `mt run`, surface `E   ` assertion lines in `GATE_VERIFY_CMD_FAILED` `Findings`, and enforce stage-scoped `Traceability` checks during `mt approve` and `mt resolve`.

## Impact Analysis
- Updates `run_scheduled_tasks` in `src/orchestrator/task_runner.py` to loop across eligible `Task` waves until completion or first `Finding`.
- Updates `_run_verification_command` in `src/orchestrator/merge_gate.py` to include the first `E   ` failure line alongside the final summary line.
- Scopes `check_traceability` in `src/checkers/traceability_checker.py` by `target_paths` and adds `traceability` validation to `approve_artifact` and `resolve_escalation` in `src/orchestrator/lifecycle.py`.
