## Statement
`group_artifacts_by_intent` shall keep per-`Intent` subgraphs disjoint across `supersedes` links while `trace_lineage` continues traversing `supersedes` relationships, and `render_target_context_section` shall inline superseded `Requirement` artifacts, their linked `Specification` artifacts, and existing `target_files` when targeting a superseding `Requirement`.

## Impact Analysis
Updates `src/multi_tasker/orchestrator/lineage.py`, `src/multi_tasker/orchestrator/prompt_context.py`, and `tests/test_lifecycle.py`.
