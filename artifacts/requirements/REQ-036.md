## Statement
`build_agent_prompt` shall instruct `RequirementAgent` via a `Requirement Decomposition Contract` to decompose `Intent` prose into singular capability `Requirement` files evaluated across good, edge, and failure `AcceptanceCriteria`, render an `Existing Requirements Catalog` when targeting an `Intent`, and mandate capability atomicity and behavioral `AcceptanceCriterion` verification in `ReviewAgent`'s `Semantic Review Rubric`.

## Impact Analysis
Updates `src/multi_tasker/orchestrator/prompt_builder.py`, `src/multi_tasker/orchestrator/prompt_context.py`, and `tests/test_orchestrator.py` so `RequirementAgent` and `ReviewAgent` enforce atomic, multi-scenario `Requirement` artifacts.
