## Statement
Support configurable CLI execution environments (such as `agy` headless mode) and model selection (such as `gemini-3.8-flash-high`) across `Policy` defaults, `Task` frontmatter, and `mt run` CLI flags.

## Impact Analysis
- Extends `policy/permissions.yaml` (`execution_environments`) and `policy/schemas.yaml` (`model`, `execution_environment`).
- Updates `src/checkers/permissions_checker.py`, `src/orchestrator/prompt_builder.py`, `src/orchestrator/lifecycle.py`, and `src/cli/main.py`.
