## Statement
Ensure `RUST_FN_REGEX`, `C_FN_REGEX`, and `C_VAR_DECL_REGEX` in `language_parser.py` use disjoint character classes and non-overlapping quantifiers so function and variable extraction executes in linear time without regular expression backtracking vulnerabilities.

## Impact Analysis
- Updates `RUST_FN_REGEX`, `C_FN_REGEX`, and `C_VAR_DECL_REGEX` in `src/multi_tasker/checkers/language_parser.py`.
- Updates `tests/test_multi_language_greenfield_hardening.py` to verify `REQ-056:AC-01` against pathological repeated-bracket inputs and valid Rust/C signatures.
