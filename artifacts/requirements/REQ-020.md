## Statement
The system must guide `Intent` authoring to state both what to change and why across `policy/glossary.yaml`, `mt intent` CLI help, and `AGENTS.md` bootstrapping instructions so `RequirementAgent` translates both the requested modification and its motivating reason into `AcceptanceCriteria` and `ImpactAnalysis`.

## Impact Analysis
- Updates `Intent` and `RequirementAgent` definitions in `policy/glossary.yaml`, enriching the `**Mission**:` line synthesized by `build_agent_prompt` for `requirement-agent`.
- Updates `_add_lifecycle_parsers` in `src/cli/main.py` and `generate_external_agents_guide` in `src/orchestrator/documentation_generator.py` (alongside repository `AGENTS.md`) to prompt for `<what to change and why>`.
