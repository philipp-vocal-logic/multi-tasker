## Statement
The `Orchestrator`, `Checker`, `domain`, and `cli` modules shall reside under `src/multi_tasker/**` and use `multi_tasker.*` imports exclusively, `pyproject.toml` shall not export top-level `src`, and `Worktree` and `MergeGate` subprocesses shall set `PYTHONPATH` to `worktree_path` (preserving any existing `PYTHONPATH`) without injecting the `multi-tasker` repository root.

## Impact Analysis
Relocates `src/{domain,checkers,orchestrator,cli}` to `src/multi_tasker/{domain,checkers,orchestrator,cli}`, updates `pyproject.toml`, `mt`, `policy/architecture.yaml`, `src/multi_tasker/**`, `tests/**`, and historical `SPEC`/`TASK` file path references to `src/multi_tasker/**`.
