## Statement
`build_agent_prompt` shall streamline `worker-agent` prompts by expanding `$TASK_WRITE_FILES` to the target Task's concrete `write_files`, embedding the concrete `mt check all ... && <verification_cmd>` command in `Execution Steps`, and omitting the `TASK` output schema and `Assigned Next Artifact ID`, while specifying target statuses (`in_review` for `REQ`/`SPEC`, `ready` for `TASK`) in `Execution Steps` for artifact roles.

## Impact Analysis
Updates `src/multi_tasker/orchestrator/prompt_builder.py` and `tests/test_interaction_logging.py`.
